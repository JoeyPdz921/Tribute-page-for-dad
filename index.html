A tribute to my father, Leonardo Pedraza

Introduction

My father, Leonardo Pedraza was born in Santa Clara, Cuba in the year 1956 to Angela Mildred Torres and Rodolfo Tiburcio Pedraza. Although he was born in Cuba, He spent a lot of his life here in America. Mr. Pedraza spent much of his life living in miami, florida where he met his best friends Michael Tuch, and Simon Heiblum. The 3 of them remained close friends until his passing in 2023. In 1987 he met his future wife Rhonda pedraza and they later got married in january 1988.

My father was a hardworking man who never met a stranger in his life. He worked as a pilot for united airlines for 30 years before going on disability leave in 2014. He loved to travel and one of his favorite places to visit was miami where he would take his kids to the beach and see family.



Mr. Pedraza had 4 kids, Carlos, Rudy, Leo, And Joey. On top of his love for family and aviation, he also shared a passion for helping people. One notable example is of a man named Brian, Who Mr. Pedraza had met in 2018 and when he first met Mr.Cooke he was quitting drugs and trying to get back on his feet. My dad took him back to our house and paid him to help around the yard.

My Father passed away on August 10th, 2023 from complications with heart failure. He is survived by His loving family and friends who continue to talk highly of him even 2 years after his passing. Mr. Pedraza is loved and missed by many.
